I am a PhD candidate in political science at the Centre for the Study of Politics (CEVIPOL) at the Université libre de Bruxelles, where I am a member of the [Social Media Lab](https://cevipol.phisoc.ulb.be/fr/projets-de-recherche/sml).

My research broadly addresses the interplay between digital media and the communication strategies of political actors. In my dissertation, I examine how such actors use social media to build collective identity and advance their agenda, focusing on a diverse set of far-right actors on X and their use of conspiratorial discourse.. Methodologically, I rely on mixed-methods designs that combine computational text and network analysis with statistical modelling and qualitative content analysis.

My work has been published in *Information, Communication & Society*, *New Media & Society*, and *Political Studies*. In 2025, I was a visiting research fellow at the Department of Journalism, Media and Communication at the University of Gothenburg and at the Centre for European Studies and Comparative Politics at Sciences Po Paris.

Research
======

**Disinformation and conspiracism.** How political actors and coordinated networks spread misleading and conspiratorial narratives that bind the in-group and carry their views beyond their core audiences.

**Political discourse across platforms.** How political discourse, particularly from the far right, travels across actors and platforms, adapting to political and platform opportunity structures.

**Platform curation.** How social media platforms structure political discourse, allocating who gets heard and when through content moderation, interface design, and recommendation systems.
